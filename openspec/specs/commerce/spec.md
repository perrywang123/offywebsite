# Commerce Specification (Reserved)

## Purpose
Future capability for selling products: catalog, cart, orders, and payment. Not implemented in the current milestone; defined here so the domain model and interfaces are agreed before implementation.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Product catalog
The system SHALL expose a catalog of products with identifiers, names, descriptions, prices, and availability. (Reserved — not yet implemented.)

### Requirement: Cart
The system SHALL allow a visitor to hold selected products in a session cart. (Reserved — not yet implemented.)

### Requirement: Orders
The system SHALL capture an order from a cart with customer and line items. (Reserved — not yet implemented.)

### Requirement: Payment
The system SHALL support pluggable payment providers behind a single interface. (Reserved — not yet implemented.)
