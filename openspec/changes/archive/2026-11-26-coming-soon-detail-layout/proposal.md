## Why

The approved preview brings the release status into the existing product information layout. The current preview repeats the opening time across the product title, notice and story, and displays the notice in UTC while the launches are announced in Eastern time.

## What Changes

- Present a compact Coming Soon label, Eastern release date and countdown below the existing product heading.
- Use the Shopify release field as the only scheduled instant and suppress recognized duplicate launch copy in the preview display.
- Keep the existing gallery order, normal product presentation, homepage cards and purchase gate.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `commerce`: refine the scheduled product preview on the normal product route.

## Impact

Product detail presentation and the detail variant of the countdown. No storage or Shopify configuration changes are required.
