## Why

The ACE card is missing from The Latest from OFFY when its product is unavailable in the visitor's Shopify market. The owner wants all five editorial cards displayed and will check their destinations when configuring them.

## What Changes

- Render the published feature and all four secondary cards in their configured order regardless of Shopify availability.
- Prompt editors to check card destinations before publishing.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `brand-site` uses homepage settings to determine which editorial cards visitors see.

## Impact

Homepage News rendering, homepage configuration guidance, and the related regression tests and specifications.
