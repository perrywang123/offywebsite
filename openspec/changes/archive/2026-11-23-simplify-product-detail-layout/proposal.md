## Why

Reduce repeated labels and disclosure controls on the Offy product page so the product, gifts and description read as one continuous layout.

## What Changes

- Remove the series label above the product name.
- Present the introduction and remaining Shopify description together below the gift and styling information with tighter spacing.
- Show eligible gift offers under a static heading.
- Pause the separate close-up section while keeping the size and materials guides available.

## Capabilities

- Refine the commerce product overview, detail photography and verified gift offer presentation.

## Impact

Product detail layout, gift presentation and typography. Shopify supplies the same product and gift data used at checkout.
