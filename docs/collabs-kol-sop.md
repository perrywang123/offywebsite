## 1. Accept your Offy invitation

Open the invitation you received from Offy and use the same email address to sign in to Shopify Collabs. Confirm that the brand is **is.offy** and review your commission rate, eligible products, audience offer and payment terms before accepting.

Collabs currently supports direct brand invitations while general new creator signups are paused. If your invite is missing or expired, contact **yifangu3@gmail.com** so Offy can check it; do not repeatedly create accounts with different emails. [Official creator account guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/creators)

You use your own creator account. You do not need access to Shopify merchant administration or the private Offy administrator system.

## 2. Activate your own automatic payouts

In the [Collabs website](https://collabs.shopify.com/), go to **Settings → Payouts → Activate auto-payouts** and follow the official onboarding steps. Complete any required identity, tax or payout information within the official platform yourself. Available methods depend on your location and the payout provider.

Check that activation is complete before your campaign starts. Offy staff will never need your password, verification code, identity document or bank details in chat. [Official payout setup](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/creators/payments)

## 3. Use the exact link and code approved for your partnership

Copy your current affiliate link and creator code from the active Offy partnership, or use the Offy short link supplied and checked by the brand. Collabs provides the official affiliate link and code; the brand creates any `isoffy.com/r/your-alias` short link in its separate Offy administrator system. Typing a new path into Collabs or changing an existing address does not create an Offy page or activate a short link. Your code, commission rate and audience offer may be different from another creator's.

Before posting, open the final link and confirm it leads to the intended Offy page. Keep the full address and its tracking parameters intact, including `dt_id` when present. Do not replace it with a normal homepage link, invent a `#name` link, or remove everything after `?`. Use the code exactly as supplied.

If you are promoting through Instagram, TikTok or another app, also check the link from that app. Ask Offy to confirm the displayed offer in Shopify checkout; an item price on the Offy product page is not a promise that every discount can be combined. Do not change your link destination, advertised discount or commission wording without checking with Offy.

Offy's short link can look like `https://isoffy.com/r/your-alias`. Only use an address actually assigned to you; the example is not an active invitation or code.

## 4. Publish, then check your own results

Place your approved link and code where your audience can use them. Make any sponsorship or affiliate disclosures required by your platform and applicable rules. Keep a copy of the published URL, publication time and link/code used, so discrepancies can be investigated.

In Collabs, open **Analytics** and locate Offy to review the creator metrics the platform provides. Distinguish visits, conversions, sales and earned commission. A visit is not an order, and earned commission is not the same as cash already paid to your bank. Refunds and cancellations may change commission.

If you compare numbers with Offy, state the period and currency shown in your account. Web Analytics may show the full partnership history rather than the brand's chosen monthly period. On the mobile app, **Performance** offers a date-range filter and information about top products and links. [Web analytics](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/creators/payments), [mobile performance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/creators/app)

Offy uses Collabs' attribution rules. It does not operate a separate promise that every click, every device or every later order must count for your account. If several links or codes were involved, ask the brand to investigate the actual platform record instead of estimating your commission from clicks.

## 5. Understand when commission becomes a payout

Offy's current holding period is **30 days**. After the hold, commission enters the merchant billing and payout process; it is not a guaranteed payment on day 30. In **Payouts**, check your pending balance, next payout and the platform's displayed schedule. The current official guidance describes twice-monthly automatic payouts once the next-payout balance reaches **USD 25**. [Official payout timing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/creators/payments)

Currency conversion may affect the amount you receive if you select a different payout currency. Check the available method and its fees in your official payout settings. [Payout methods](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/manage-account/manage-payouts-invoices/payout-method)

For illustration only: if an eligible USD 45.90 item earns 10% commission with no discount or adjustment, the example commission is **USD 4.59**. If your eligible base were USD 41.31 and your rate 7.5%, the example would be approximately **USD 3.10**. Your active terms, eligible products, discounts, adjustments and platform rounding determine the actual amount. These examples do not set your rate or guarantee that a specific order qualifies.

If Collabs shows **Waiting for creator**, finish your payout setup. If payment appears scheduled or paid but has not arrived, check the official payment record and receiving method, then contact Offy or platform support with its reference. Do not request a second informal payment for a commission still going through Collabs.

## 6. Report a link, order or commission problem

Email **yifangu3@gmail.com** with a short description and only the information needed to investigate:

- Your creator account email or identifier and the affected Offy partnership.
- The link/code used, publication channel, approximate time and time zone.
- The order number, if you have permission to share it, and the currency or amount in question.
- The relevant Collabs status or a screenshot with unrelated personal information removed.

Do not send full payment-card numbers, passwords, verification codes or customers' private addresses. If a shopper fails before entering an email, ask them to retain the checkout URL and approximate time privately; send sensitive checkout details only through the support route Offy confirms.

If your code stops working or a partnership is paused, contact Offy before publishing new promotional claims. Existing payable commission and the status of future promotions are separate matters. A missing Offy report screenshot does not replace the official information in your Collabs account.

## Before your first campaign

Before public launch, confirm these four items with Offy: **accepted partnership, correct rate and offer, activated payouts, and the exact approved working link/code**. Offy coordinates any approved small pilot and checks the first real conversion with you before expanding promotion. A working page or unpaid checkout is not proof of an attributed sale or commission. You are not required to buy a product yourself or give anyone access to your account. Offy's administrator keeps a separate per-creator launch checklist; your confirmation should reflect what you can actually see in your own account.
